# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Iris Species Prediction project welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Everyone should be able to ask questions, give feedback, and contribute free from harassment or discrimination. We expect participants to respect differences in identity, background, ability, and perspective.

## How we work together

- Welcome questions and explain decisions in terms that other contributors can follow.
- Focus feedback on the code, analysis, or proposal. Explain concerns and suggest ways to address them.
- Listen to differing perspectives, acknowledge mistakes, and give credit for others' contributions.
- Ask permission before sharing someone else's personal information or private messages.

Harassment, discrimination, bullying, threats, slurs, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern or helps address one is also unacceptable.

## Where this applies

This code applies to project collaboration in GitHub issues, pull requests, and reviews, as well as in Slack, meetings, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## How we respond

The response depends on the seriousness and impact of the conduct and whether it has happened before. Maintainers may explain the concern privately and ask for a correction, issue a formal warning, remove harmful content, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation in project spaces. If the issue involves course participation or requires action beyond the maintainers' authority, the team will refer it to the teaching team.
